Overview

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet in the Solar System. Because it has almost no atmosphere, temperatures change drastically between day and night.

Key Facts

Distance from Sun: 57.9 million km
Diameter: 4,879 km
Length of Day: 59 Earth days
Length of Year: 88 Earth days
Moons: None

Interesting Facts

Mercury has the shortest year in the Solar System.
Temperatures range from −180°C to 430°C.
The surface is covered with thousands of impact craters.
